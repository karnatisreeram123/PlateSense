The Challenge

Why is food being wasted?

College canteens lack a simple, data-driven way to estimate how much food to prepare for each meal, leading to avoidable waste and increased operating costs.

Over-Preparation

Canteens often estimate portions using habit or rough attendance, resulting in cooking significantly more food than needed.

Uncertain Demand

Attendance changes drastically by day, specific menu items, weather, and shifting campus schedules.

Hidden Costs

Leftovers aren't just wasted food; they directly increase ingredient costs, waste labour hours, and inflate waste-handling expenses.

Our Solution

A Practical Workflow

Start with a simple form and dashboard. No expensive hardware is required for the pilot. We turn food-waste data into actionable prep decisions.

1

Record

Track expected meal attendance, portions prepared, and measure leftovers by specific menu items after service.

2

Learn

Our system compares baseline data to identify attendance patterns, popular dishes, and recurring sources of waste.

3

Recommend

PlateSense suggests an exact, optimized preparation quantity for the next meal. Kitchen staff review and make the final call.
